# Self-hosted Personal AI Assistants

**Last Updated:** 2026-11-13

Open-source assistants and bot platforms you run on your own machines (or self-host), instead of a hosted “team of bots” service.

- **[OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw)** — MIT personal AI assistant that runs on your hardware and meets you in Discord, iMessage, Slack, Teams, Telegram, WhatsApp, and 20+ other channels, plus native apps for macOS, iOS, Android, Windows, and Linux. Models and harnesses (Claude, Codex, local models) are swappable plugins; no paid tier or hosted service. Stewarded by the OpenClaw Foundation. Site: [openclaw.ai](https://openclaw.ai). Docs: [docs.openclaw.ai](https://docs.openclaw.ai). Install (macOS/Linux/WSL2): `curl -fsSL https://openclaw.ai/install.sh | bash`. Windows: `iwr -useb https://openclaw.ai/install.ps1 | iex`. macOS app: `brew install --cask openclaw` (macOS 26+). Or `npm install -g openclaw@latest`.
- **[OpenMausBot](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot)** — Open-source, local-first alternative to Grok Bot (also to Meta Muse, OpenAI dots, and Cue by Manus): a chat app of real agents (Claude, Codex, or Grok CLI) with their own model, computer, and connected apps, plus approval before they act. macOS (Apple silicon and Intel, signed/notarized), Windows, and Ubuntu desktop builds. Site: [openmausbot.com](https://www.openmausbot.com). Download: [openmausbot.com/download](https://www.openmausbot.com/download) or [releases](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot/releases/latest).
- **[Rakazo](https://github.com/elie222/rakazo)** — Open-source, self-hosted alternative to Grok Bot (also to Muse, Dots, OpenClaw, and others): persistent AI teammates with memory, routines, computer/browser use, sandboxes, group chats, voice, and MCP, on the models and keys you bring. Web, desktop, iPhone, and Android clients talk to the same server. Site: [rakazo.com](https://rakazo.com). Desktop: [rakazo.com/download](https://rakazo.com/download/). Self-host (Docker): `mkdir -p rakazo && cd rakazo && curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elie222/rakazo/main/infra/compose/install-images.sh && bash install-images.sh`, then open http://127.0.0.1:5173.
